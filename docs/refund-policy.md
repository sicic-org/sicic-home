# Course Bookings

Bookings for **{{ course_name }}** are governed in full by our [Booking Terms & Cancellation Policy](booking-terms.md), which supersede any earlier workshop refund policy.

In summary, those terms give you:

- a **statutory right to cancel within 14 days** of your booking confirmation, without giving a reason (section 5)
- a **full refund at any time before the second session begins** — whether you cancel weeks ahead, the day before your cohort starts, or after attending the first session (section 6)
- a **50% refund** if you withdraw after the second session and before the end of the final Week 1 session (section 6)
- the option to **rejoin a later cohort free** within 12 months if you cannot complete (section 6)
- a **full refund** if we do not reach the minimum cohort size, or if we materially change your cohort's dates or timings (section 8)

Where these guarantees and your legal rights would produce different outcomes, whichever is more favourable to you applies. Cancellations and refund requests go to [{{ register_email }}](mailto:{{ register_email }}).

Different rules apply where an employer or other organisation books and pays for a place — see section 15 of the [Booking Terms](booking-terms.md#15-business-customers).

# Membership Refund Policy

## 1. Non-refundable nature of membership

All SICIC membership fees are non-refundable upon payment. Membership is a lifetime access entitlement that takes effect immediately upon confirmation of payment. By completing payment, the member acknowledges that the membership service has commenced and expressly consents to the waiver of any applicable cancellation right.

## 2. Basis under UK Consumer Rights Act 2015

Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 and the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013, consumers ordinarily have a 14-day right to cancel distance contracts. However, this right does not apply where:

- the service has been fully performed
- the consumer has given prior express consent to performance beginning before the cancellation period has expired and acknowledged that the right to cancel will be lost

By proceeding with payment, the member provides such consent and acknowledgement.

## 3. Pre-purchase evaluation

SICIC membership is accessible only through an invitation-only process that requires prior completion of a paid course. Members have therefore had a meaningful opportunity to evaluate the community, curriculum, and culture before committing to membership. No refund will be issued on the basis that expectations were not met.

## 4. Exceptional circumstances

SICIC reserves the right to consider refund requests in exceptional circumstances at its sole discretion. Such requests must be submitted in writing to [{{ general_email }}](mailto:{{ general_email }}) within 7 days of payment. The decision of SICIC's directors shall be final.

## 5. Membership transfer

Membership is personal and non-transferable. It may not be assigned, gifted, or sold to another individual.

## 6. Consumer rights

Nothing in this policy affects your statutory rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 or the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013.
