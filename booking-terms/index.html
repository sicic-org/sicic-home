Booking Terms & Cancellation Policy¶

Introduction to AI Agent Building in the Wild — Super Individuals CIC

Last updated: August 2026

These terms reflect UK consumer law as at August 2026.

1. Who we are and what these terms cover¶

We are Super Individuals CIC, a community interest company registered in England and Wales, company number 17248930, registered office at 11 Third Avenue, Stoke, Plymouth, United Kingdom. You can contact us at register@sicic.org.

These terms apply when you book a place on Introduction to AI Agent Building in the Wild ("the programme"). Please read them before booking. By completing a booking you confirm that you accept these terms.

If you are booking as a consumer (for purposes wholly or mainly outside your trade, business, craft or profession), you have legal rights that these terms do not affect. If your employer or another organisation is paying, section 15 applies instead.

2. What the programme is¶

The programme is a live, online, cohort-based course delivered over two weeks, comprising two modules:

  • Module 1 — Intro Track: deploying and securing an AI agent on infrastructure you control, agent identity and behaviour design, and publishing a documentation portfolio.
  • Module 2 — Builder Track: node-based orchestration, connecting an autonomous agent runtime, and designing an MVP architecture for your own system.

Sessions run on weekday evenings (18:00–21:00 UK time) and Saturdays (10:00–17:00 UK time), across 8 guided learning days. Each day combines a scheduled session — an introductory meeting, an optional office-hours surgery, or a facilitated group call — with guided self-directed learning supported through our Discord community.

The programme comprises 30 hours of structured, directed learning. Live sessions are recorded and made available to participants during the programme.

Cohorts are capped at 15 participants and require a minimum of 12 to run. Section 8 explains what happens if we do not reach the minimum.

3. Booking and payment¶

Your booking is a request to reserve a place. A contract between us is formed when we send you a booking confirmation by email. We will send that confirmation, together with a copy of these terms and the cancellation form at Annex A, in a form you can keep.

Payment is due in full at the time of booking unless we agree otherwise in writing. Places are confirmed on receipt of payment.

If a cohort is full, we will tell you and offer you a place on the waiting list or the next available cohort.

5. Your legal right to cancel¶

You have the right to cancel this contract within 14 days without giving any reason.

The cancellation period expires 14 days from the day after the contract is concluded (that is, the day after we send your booking confirmation).

How to cancel. To exercise the right to cancel, you must inform us of your decision by a clear statement — for example, a letter sent by post or an email — to:

Super Individuals CIC, 11 Third Avenue, Stoke, Plymouth, United Kingdom Email: register@sicic.org

You may use the model cancellation form at Annex A, but you do not have to. To meet the cancellation deadline, it is enough that you send your communication before the cancellation period expires.

Effect of cancellation. If you cancel this contract, we will reimburse all payments received from you. We will make the reimbursement without undue delay, and no later than 14 days after the day on which we are informed of your decision to cancel. We will use the same means of payment you used, and you will not incur any fees as a result of the reimbursement.

If you asked us to start during the cancellation period. If the programme begins during your 14-day cancellation period at your express request, and you then cancel before it has been fully performed, you must pay us an amount in proportion to what has been supplied up to the point you told us you wished to cancel, compared with the full coverage of the contract. We will refund the balance.

When this right ends. If the programme begins during your cancellation period at your express request, and you have acknowledged that you will lose your right to cancel once we have fully performed the contract, your right to cancel ends when the programme has been fully performed (see section 7).

6. Our additional guarantees¶

These guarantees are offered in addition to your legal rights. Where these guarantees and your legal rights would produce different outcomes, whichever is more favourable to you applies.

Full refund at any time before the second session begins. Tell us at any point before your cohort's second session starts and we will refund you in full, with no questions asked. This applies whether you cancel weeks in advance, cancel the day before your cohort starts, or attend the first session and decide the programme is not right for you.

Telling us early does not change what you get back, but it does let us offer your place to someone on the waiting list — so please tell us as soon as you know.

Withdraw before the end of Week 1: 50% refund. If you withdraw at any point after the second session has begun and before the end of the final Week 1 session, we will refund 50% of the fee you paid. (At that point half of the programme's structured hours have been delivered.)

If life gets in the way, rejoin free. If you are unable to complete the programme, you may join a later cohort at no additional cost, subject to availability, within 12 months of your original cohort's start date. This can be used once. This is offered as a goodwill arrangement: it is not part of the contracted service and does not extend the period over which we perform this contract (see section 7).

Refunds under this section are made within 14 days of us agreeing them, to the payment method you used.

7. When the programme is fully performed¶

The programme is fully performed at the conclusion of the final scheduled session of Module 2.

Benefits that may continue after that point — continued access to our Discord community, availability of session recordings, the rejoin arrangement in section 6, and the API cost credit — are provided as goodwill. They do not form part of the contracted service and do not extend the period over which we perform this contract.

8. If we change or cancel a cohort¶

Minimum numbers. A cohort runs only if it reaches a minimum of 12 participants. We will confirm whether your cohort is going ahead no later than 7 days before the scheduled start date. If we do not reach the minimum, we will offer you the choice of a place on the next scheduled cohort or a full refund, and we will refund you within 14 days if that is what you choose.

Changes to dates or timings. If we need to change the date of your cohort or make a significant change to the scheduled session times, we will tell you as soon as possible. You may then either accept the change, move to another cohort, or cancel and receive a full refund.

Minor changes. We may make minor changes to the curriculum, the tools used, or the facilitators delivering sessions — for example, where a third-party service changes or a facilitator is unavailable. We will not make changes that materially reduce what the programme delivers.

Rescheduling individual sessions. Occasionally a single session may need to be rescheduled. Where that happens we will give as much notice as we can, and a recording will be made available.

9. What we ask of you¶

To take part you need basic computer literacy, a valid email address, a computer capable of running a web browser and a terminal, and a reliable internet connection. No prior programming or server administration experience is required.

We ask that you engage respectfully with facilitators and other participants, and follow our community guidelines in Discord. We may remove a participant from the programme for seriously or persistently unacceptable behaviour, including harassment of others or sharing another participant's private material without permission. Where we do so, we will refund you on the basis set out in section 6 as if you had withdrawn on that date, unless the circumstances make that inappropriate.

You are responsible for the security of your own accounts and for costs you incur with third-party providers beyond the credit described in section 4.

10. Certificate and CPD hours¶

On completion of the programme you will receive a certificate evidencing 30 hours of structured continuing professional development. Completion means attending or catching up on the scheduled sessions and submitting the required work.

CPD hours are calculated on structured, directed learning time only, at a ratio of one CPD hour to one hour of structured learning. Most participants spend additional time in applied practice; that time is not counted.

Super Individuals CIC is a member organisation of The CPD Certification Service (Membership No. 22924).

This programme's accreditation submission is currently under assessment by The CPD Certification Service. We will confirm the outcome to all participants. If accreditation is not in place for your cohort, we will tell you before your cohort begins, and you may cancel and receive a full refund if that materially affects your reason for booking.

The certificate is evidence of professional development. It is not a regulated qualification and does not confer a professional licence.
